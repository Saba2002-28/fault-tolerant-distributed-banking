Phase 5 — Inject the coordinator interruption
kubectl exec -n banking-assignment3 <gateway-pod-name> -- python -c "import json,urllib.request,urllib.error; u='http://localhost:5000/accounts/transfer'; d={'from_account_id':'A0999','to_account_id':'A1999','amount':100.00}; r=urllib.request.Request(u,data=json.dumps(d).encode(),headers={'Content-Type':'application/json'},method='POST'); exec(\"try:\\n x=urllib.request.urlopen(r,timeout=40); print('HTTP',x.status); print(x.read().decode())\\nexcept urllib.error.HTTPError as e:\\n print('HTTP',e.code); print(e.read().decode())\\nexcept Exception as e:\\n print(type(e).__name__+':',e)\")"
